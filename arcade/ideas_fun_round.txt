Ghost Light Mine | First-person in an old mine maze (wire walls fade past 3 cells); keys: arrows only. A floating stick-figure ghost with fading see-through copies trailing behind it drifts through the tunnels; your lantern keeps it back, but the lantern burns down and oil flasks lie in dead ends. Each level has one blockage and the thing that clears it: level 1 a key for an iron bar gate, level 2 a pickaxe for a broken wall, level 3 dynamite for a cave-in. Find the tool, reach the blockage, get out. | columns: maze size cells (9 to 17), lantern steps (60 down to 35), oil flasks (4 down to 1), ghost speed (0.5 to 1.2)
Two Key Doors | Top-down maze for the two heroes of PROMPT and GLAZE; keys: arrows, TAB to swap hero (the other follows). A red door opens when the red hero walks into it, a blue door for the blue hero. A pressure plate holds a door open while either hero stands on it: one holds, the other passes. Timed doors from level 3. The white exit needs both heroes. | columns: plates (1 to 4), timed doors (0 to 3), maze size cells (9 to 15)
Echo Rescue | Top-down haunted hallway of many doors; keys: arrows only. Lost survivors wander the rooms; walk into one and it follows you home. Sentries patrol, and every door leads somewhere you did not expect: you go in one door and come out of another down the hall, and so do the sentries and the survivors, so everyone keeps popping out in the wrong place. A sentry touching a survivor sends it back where it was found; a sentry touching you costs a life. | columns: survivors (2 to 6), sentries (1 to 5), sentry speed (60 to 100), doors (4 to 12)
One Cent Raise | Top-down warehouse that never ends; keys: arrows only, the worker pushes boxes by walking into them. Stick-figure delivery men walk in carrying wobbly towers of coloured boxes, drop them wherever it is most in the way, and walk off. Push each box into its own coloured storage room; clearing a whole room of its colour stops the clock for 5 seconds and no deliveries come. Some boxes go to truck bays off the screen edges: push one out of one side and you come back in on the other side empty-handed. Some boxes must be smashed, and some hold the letters V I B E: line them up for a big bonus. Slow-time boxes are buried behind others, worth saving for a bad moment. Points for each box are divided by the clock, so speed pays. Every 10 deliveries cleared the screen says PAY RAISE 1 CENT! | columns: deliveries a minute (2 to 6), colours (2 to 5), truck bays (0 to 3), slow-time boxes (3 down to 1)
Ring Arena | Top-down small round arena; keys: arrows to move, Space to release a shockwave. Stand still to charge the shockwave, moving cancels the charge; drones drift in from the edge and each wave is faster. Clearing a wave scores 100 times the level. | columns: drones (3 to 11), drone speed (1.5 to 2.3), lives (3 down to 1)
Wire Sentries | Top-down maze; keys: arrows only. Walk onto a data core to take it. Sentries walk fixed loops and see straight along their row or column; being seen sends you back to the last checkpoint pad, and the third time you are caught the run ends. | columns: cores (1 to 5), sentries (1 to 6), sentry speed (1.5 to 3)
Dont Break The Walls | Dark maze of glass, top-down; keys: arrows only. You are clumsy: you smash through any wall you bump into with a crash of glass, and every broken wall costs a point. The score starts at the number of walls. Only the outer walls are stone. Speed-up, slow-down and grow-bigger mushrooms lie in the maze (bigger means you break two walls at once). It is dark, so you only see the walls next to you and one layer beyond. Find the stone at the end of the maze, because people who live in glass houses should not throw stones. | columns: maze size cells (7 to 15), mushrooms (2 to 6), light radius cells (2 down to 1)
Hollow Mountain | Side view of a mountain cut into floors of horizontal lines with holes to drop through; keys: arrows to walk and drop, Space for the flashlight. A ghost drifts through walls and floors after you; the flashlight beam scares it off but drains the battery. Spiders hang on threads in the holes. Find the diamond at the bottom and climb back out. Secret: a miner who dances left-right-left on the bottom floor long enough slips through the floor into a glitched world of garbled shapes worth strange points. | columns: floors (5 to 12), spiders (1 to 6), ghost speed (0.5 to 1.5), battery seconds (30 down to 15)
Courier Run | Top-down city maze with wide streets; keys: arrows only. Pick up numbered parcels at the depot and deliver each by walking into its numbered door before the clock runs out; dogs chase you. Fast deliveries earn tips; tip coins lie in the street. Some customers shout "That's not what I ordered. Send it back." and you carry it back to the warehouse for a few points or sell it at the pawn shop for more. | columns: parcels (3 to 8), dogs (1 to 5), dog speed (1 to 3), clock seconds (60 down to 40)
UFO Wrong Cargo | Side-scrolling flight over a midwest farm, ranch house and trailer park; keys: arrows to fly, Space for the tractor beam. Opening: the UFO flies across the land and stick-figure people tumble out of its back hatch; it turns around and says "Damn it, not again!" You must hover to beam people back up (not cows: a cow is wrong cargo and costs points) while the locals, then the police, then the army shoot at you; teams in squad buggies drive in to rescue them. | columns: people to collect (5 to 15), buggies (1 to 5), shots a second (1 to 4), cows (2 to 8)
Fellout | Turn-based strategy seen from above at an angle, same story as UFO Wrong Cargo: the UFO dropped its stick-figure abductees over a farm and trailer park and must get them back. Each round the UFO has action points to spend: move a cell, hover, beam up a person, shield; then the locals, police and army take their turn and fire. Beaming takes a whole round of hovering in the open. | columns: action points (6 down to 4), people (4 to 10), enemy units (2 to 8), cover pieces (8 down to 3)
UFO Low Pass | Third-person chase view behind the UFO flying low over the same farm and trailer park, same story as UFO Wrong Cargo; keys: arrows to steer and climb, Space for the tractor beam. Flying low makes the beam reach but brings you into range of the guns; power lines and water towers stand in the way. | columns: people to collect (5 to 15), guns (2 to 10), power lines (0 to 6), cows (2 to 8)
