NOTES
- Level 1 has a small meadow with few fireflies and long lit periods, giving a relaxed start.
- Level 2 adds more fireflies and a wider meadow, requiring more careful movement between blinking targets.
- Level 3 narrows lit seconds and increases firefly count, making timing more difficult.
- Level 4 and 5 use the smallest lit seconds and the largest meadow, demanding precise navigation without missing dark fireflies.
