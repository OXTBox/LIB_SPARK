NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use low wind strength and fewer windows, giving the player simple steering control.
- Levels 3 to 5 increase wind strength and window count, while roof gap cells grow to require more precise steering.
- Level 5 combines maximum wind strength and the most windows, making the fast automatic rightward movement harder to manage.
