NOTES
- Level 1 uses a small yard with slow wind to let the player learn steady pushing and leaf collection.
- Level 2 adds more leaves and a slightly stronger wind to require more careful pushing while avoiding scattered leaves.
- Level 3 increases leaf count and wind frequency to make balancing pushing and wind control more difficult.
- Level 4 raises wind speed and leaf quantity to demand precise control of pushing and leaf collection timing.
- Level 5 uses the largest yard with the highest wind frequency to make building the full pile while avoiding scattered leaves the hardest.
