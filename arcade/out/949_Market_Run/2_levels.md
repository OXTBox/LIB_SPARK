NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base market speed and 3 lives, with a simple layout where shoppers are spaced far apart to reduce path-blocking risk.
- Level 2 keeps the same speed and item count but adds one moving shopper near the central stalls, requiring the player to navigate around the block while buying items in order.
- Level 3 reduces lives to 2 and raises the market speed slightly, making the timed shopping list more urgent while shoppers are denser across the street.
- Level 4 keeps 2 lives but increases the market speed to 140 and adds a second shopper cluster near the exit, forcing tighter navigation to complete the items before the market closes.
- Level 5 is the hardest, with 1 life, market speed 160, and the densest shopper placement, requiring quick, precise navigation to buy all items in order before the market closes.
