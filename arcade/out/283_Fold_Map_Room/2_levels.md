NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single fold line with no locked walls, so the player can reach the exit using the default path after moving.
- Level 2 adds one locked wall that requires picking up the purple key first to pass, increasing the spatial planning needed.
- Level 3 adds a second fold line and a switch that must be activated by folding, requiring the player to solve both fold lines before reaching the exit.
- Level 4 increases the wall density and moves limit, forcing the player to plan fold sequences carefully to avoid exceeding the move count.
- Level 5 combines all mechanics with higher wall density and a tighter move limit, making the fold and collision logic the main challenge.
