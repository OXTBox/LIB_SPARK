NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest: one rival, no obstacles, and full lives to guide the player across the table.
- Level 2 adds a single crumb obstacle that forces the player to steer around it for the first time.
- Level 3 adds a second crumb and a pencil obstacle, increasing the number of obstacles to dodge quickly.
- Level 4 adds a juice spill hazard that costs more time, requiring faster steering to avoid it.
- Level 5 adds five obstacles including the juice spill and a tighter layout, requiring the fastest possible reactions to win.
