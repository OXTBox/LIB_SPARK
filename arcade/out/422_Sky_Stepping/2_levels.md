NOTES
- Level 1 uses three slabs with slow drift and three lives, letting the player focus on basic slab walking without pressure.
- Level 2 adds one more slab and slightly faster drift, requiring the player to manage slab movement while collecting tokens.
- Level 3 reduces lives to two and increases slab count, raising the pressure of surviving sinking slabs during movement.
- Level 4 increases slab count to six and keeps reduced lives, demanding precise platform navigation to avoid sinking slabs.
- Level 5 uses the maximum slab count of seven with the fastest drift and only one life, making slab management the core challenge of the final level.
