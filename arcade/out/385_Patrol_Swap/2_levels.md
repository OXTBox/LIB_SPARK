NOTES
- Level 1 uses a small open yard with one searchlight and a slow light to let players learn the swap mechanic without pressure.
- Level 2 adds a tighter corridor layout that limits safe movement space, making it harder to plan swaps without risking the light.
- Level 3 introduces a second searchlight to increase the pressure of the light following the last mover while the maze gets denser.
- Level 4 adds narrow gaps and multiple searchlights to force careful timing of swaps and movement through tight spaces.
- Level 5 combines the largest maze, the most searchlights, and the fastest light to require precise planning of every move to reach the gate safely.
