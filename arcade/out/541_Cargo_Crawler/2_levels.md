NOTES
- Level 1 is the simplest layout with wide open aisles, giving the player easy control to pull the single crate and reach the dock.
- Level 2 adds a small internal wall block that narrows the path, requiring careful movement to avoid the chain crossing itself.
- Level 3 introduces a tighter maze of internal walls that restrict movement space, making chain self-crossing more likely.
- Level 4 increases wall density and narrows the main aisles further, forcing precise navigation to keep the chain unbroken.
- Level 5 combines the highest wall density with the tightest dock area, making the fixed forklift loop interaction the main challenge for the player.
