NOTES
- Level 1 is a simple open corridor with one sentry moving slowly, giving clear movement and core collection practice.
- Level 2 adds a second sentry on a parallel loop, increasing the need to dodge while navigating the maze.
- Level 3 raises sentry speed and adds a third sentry with longer sight lines, making straight corridor movement riskier.
- Level 4 increases sentry count to four and extends sight lines further, requiring faster evasive movement through tighter corridors.
- Level 5 raises sentry speed to the highest and adds five sentries with the longest sight lines, demanding constant quick movement to reach all cores safely.
