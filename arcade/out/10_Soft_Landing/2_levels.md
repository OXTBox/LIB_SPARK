NOTES
- Level 1 uses the standard starting fuel and the lowest landing speed, giving the player a clear baseline to learn the control mechanics.
- Level 2 widens the pad and raises the max landing speed, adding a small precision requirement while keeping fuel ample for safe adjustments.
- Level 3 increases the required landing speed and narrows the effective landing zone, making precise vertical control more demanding.
- Level 4 cuts fuel availability while raising the landing speed, forcing the player to manage limited engine shots carefully during the descent.
- Level 5 combines the highest landing speed and the most constrained fuel, requiring full mastery of thrust and movement to land softly on the pad.
