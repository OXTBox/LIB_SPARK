NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple open floor with one small wall block to teach the pebble trail and basic movement.
- Level 2 adds a second wall column to require more careful path planning while dropping more pebbles.
- Level 3 introduces a third wall column with a central obstacle, increasing maze complexity and pebble count.
- Level 4 adds a fourth wall column with tighter corridors, making pebble placement harder and movement more constrained.
- Level 5 adds a fifth wall column with dense obstacles and the most pebbles, making the trail planning and exit gating the hardest challenge.
