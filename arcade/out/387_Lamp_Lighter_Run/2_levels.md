NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base walking speed with 5 lamps and 3 lives, giving a relaxed start to learn the arrow controls and lamp lighting.
- Level 2 adds one more lamp and a slightly faster walking speed, making the steady forward movement slightly more demanding.
- Level 3 increases the lamp count and reduces lives to 2, raising the pressure to light all lamps before shadows disrupt the street.
- Level 4 raises the lamp count and keeps lives at 2 with a faster walking speed, requiring tighter coordination between walking and shadow avoidance.
- Level 5 adds the final lamp, keeps lives at 1, and uses the highest walking speed, making the shadow drift and lamp lighting interaction the hardest challenge.
