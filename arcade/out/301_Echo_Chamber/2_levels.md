NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest start: the chamber is small, targets are few, and the time limit is generous for learning the collection movement.
- Level 2 adds one more target and slightly tighter time pressure, requiring the player to collect targets faster while staying inside the chamber.
- Level 3 raises the player speed limit and reduces lives, adding more targets that must be collected before the shorter time limit expires.
- Level 4 further increases target count and lowers lives, with a tighter time limit that demands quick, focused collection of all targets.
- Level 5 is the hardest: it has the lowest player speed, the fewest lives, the most targets, and the shortest time limit, requiring constant fast navigation to collect every target before time runs out.
