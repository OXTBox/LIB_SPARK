NOTES
- Level 1 uses a short quay with one ship at a slow speed for simple timing practice.
- Level 2 adds a second ship and a wider quay to build steady rhythm control.
- Level 3 increases the number of ships and faster speeds to demand quick flag matching.
- Level 4 tightens the quay layout and raises ship speeds to raise timing pressure.
- Level 5 uses the full set of berths with the fastest ship speeds for the final challenge.
