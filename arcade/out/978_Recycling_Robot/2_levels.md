LEVELS
level | name | litter at start (5 to 15) | carry limit (3 down to 2) | wind seconds (20 down to 8)

4 | Busy Corridor | 15 | 2 | 12
5 | Final Sorting | 15 | 2 | 8
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a wide open street with full carry capacity and slow wind to let the player learn the sorting mechanic.
- Level 2 adds more litter items and slightly faster wind to increase the pressure of managing the street.
- Level 3 reduces the carry limit to force the player to sort litter more quickly and carefully.
- Level 4 increases litter density and wind speed to make the sorting task harder to complete.
- Level 5 combines maximum litter density with the slowest wind interval to test full sorting skill under constant pressure.
