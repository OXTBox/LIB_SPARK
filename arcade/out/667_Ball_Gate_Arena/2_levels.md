NOTES
- Level 1 has no drones and a single lit gate, so the player learns to push the ball through the portal without any obstacles.
- Level 2 adds one slow drone to test basic path avoidance while the gate layout stays simple.
- Level 3 increases drone count and speed, forcing the player to time gate entry and drone paths more carefully.
- Level 4 raises drone density and speed, making the path between gates harder to navigate safely.
- Level 5 combines the highest goal count with the fastest drones, requiring precise coordination of ball movement and gate timing.
