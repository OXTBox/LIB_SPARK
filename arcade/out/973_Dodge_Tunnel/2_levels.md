NOTES
- Level 1 uses the lowest incoming speed and the most lives to let the player learn arrow and space controls.
- Level 2 raises the incoming speed and adds more cubes to require smoother slide moves.
- Level 3 increases cube count and lowers lives to force players to use climb moves against low incoming cubes.
- Level 4 raises speed and cube count further while keeping only two lives to demand precise corner dodges.
- Level 5 uses the highest speed and lowest lives with the most cubes to require constant reactive dodging through the final wave.
