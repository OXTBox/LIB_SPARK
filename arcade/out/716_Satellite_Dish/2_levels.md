NOTES
- Level 1 has no clouds and a slow satellite so the player can focus on basic aiming.
- Level 2 adds a few clouds and a faster satellite to require early signal management.
- Level 3 adds a second satellite to track while avoiding clouds, increasing aim complexity.
- Level 4 and 5 raise both satellite speed and cloud count, making signal maintenance and dodging much harder.
