NOTES
- Level 1 uses a flat terrain layout with a single gap marker and the lowest scroll speed, making leg-flap control the main focus.
- Level 2 adds a second terrain block inside the scroll path, requiring the player to break ground while maintaining flight.
- Level 3 raises the gap height and reduces ship parts to two, demanding precise leg-flap timing to stay airborne.
- Level 4 lowers ship parts to one and increases scroll speed, making terrain destruction and flight control much harder to balance.
- Level 5 combines the highest scroll speed, largest gap height, and only one ship part, requiring full flight capability to win.
