NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single falling shape at the slowest speed, giving clear reaction time for basic movement and rotation.
- Level 2 adds a second shape kind, increasing collision complexity while keeping the fall speed only slightly higher.
- Level 3 raises the required rows to clear and adds a third shape kind, tightening the path and raising difficulty.
- Level 4 increases the fall speed and row requirement further, with all three shape kinds present, making the grid navigation more demanding.
- Level 5 sets the highest fall speed and largest row requirement, with all shape kinds combined, requiring precise grid movement and rotation timing throughout.
