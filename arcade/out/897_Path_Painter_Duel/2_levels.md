NOTES
- Level 1 uses a wide open arena with no internal walls, giving the player simple movement and colouring space to build enclosed areas.
- Level 2 adds two small internal wall clusters that restrict safe movement paths, making enclosed area detection slightly more complex.
- Level 3 increases the rival hero speed to 150 and adds more wall clusters, tightening the arena and demanding faster colouring to out-colour the rival.
- Level 4 raises rival speed to 150, reduces lives to 2, and adds deeper wall clusters that trap movement routes, increasing the difficulty of enclosing areas safely.
- Level 5 raises rival speed to 180, keeps lives at 2, and adds a more complex wall layout with deeper enclosed regions, requiring fast colouring and careful wall avoidance to win.
