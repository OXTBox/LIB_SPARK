LEVELS
level | name | river width cells (4 to 10) | sink seconds (4 down to 1.5) | stones (6 to 16)
1 | River Start | 4 | 4 | 6
2 | Gentle Leaps | 6 | 3.5 | 9
3 | Synchronized Swings | 8 | 3 | 12
4 | Tight Timing | 10 | 2.5 | 15
5 | Sinking Duet | 10 | 1.5 | 16
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a narrow river and slow sinking to let players learn the leap pattern.
- Level 2 widens the river and shortens the sink time to force quicker movement.
- Level 3 adds more stones while keeping the river wide, requiring tighter position syncing.
- Level 4 narrows the safe stone spacing, demanding fast timing between the two players.
- Level 5 combines the widest river and fastest sink, making synchronized movement the hardest challenge.
