NOTES
- Level 1 uses straight vertical shafts with simple door placements, letting both heroes move freely once swapped.
- Level 2 adds a partial wall block in the middle shaft, forcing heroes to swap earlier to reach doors.
- Level 3 introduces staggered floor gaps that require precise timing of hero swaps to avoid falling.
- Level 4 increases shaft complexity with multiple partial walls and offset door positions, demanding careful coordination.
- Level 5 combines all previous challenges with tighter gaps and harder door placements, requiring full team coordination to win.
