NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single shield ring with slow movement and a simple layout, making the first probe throws easy to control.
- Level 2 adds a second shield ring that shifts faster, forcing quicker and more careful spear throws to reach the boss.
- Level 3 increases the shield ring update speed and adds more shield cubes, making the dynamic ring harder to navigate around.
- Level 4 introduces a third shield ring with faster movement and denser shield placement, requiring more precise spear timing to avoid losing lives.
- Level 5 has the fastest shield ring movement and the densest shield layout, making the boss path hardest to reach without losing lives.
