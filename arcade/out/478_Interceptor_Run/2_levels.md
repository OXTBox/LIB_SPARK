NOTES
- Level 1 uses the slowest speed and the simplest layout with only one obstacle, making the basic steering and shooting flow easy to learn.
- Level 2 adds a single mid-road obstacle to the layout while raising the scroll speed, increasing the challenge of avoiding civilian cars and enemy vehicles.
- Level 3 adds two mid-road obstacles and raises the speed further, requiring faster reaction to dodge enemies and supply trucks.
- Level 4 adds three mid-road obstacles and raises the speed even more, making the road harder to navigate while managing bomb helicopters.
- Level 5 raises the speed to the highest and adds obstacles closer to the road end, demanding steady control while approaching the water transformation zone.
