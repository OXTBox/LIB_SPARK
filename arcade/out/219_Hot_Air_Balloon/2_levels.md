NOTES
- Level 1 uses a wide, gentle valley with few pads and slow wind for easy control.
- Level 2 adds a few more pads and a slightly stronger wind to require more careful height management.
- Level 3 introduces steeper terrain gaps that demand precise up and down balancing.
- Level 4 raises the wind speed and adds more demanding terrain to increase navigation difficulty.
- Level 5 combines the highest wind speed with the most pads, making safe landing the hardest challenge.
