NOTES
- Level 1 has no ghost delay, so the player can follow the floor trail safely and reach the exit with a simple straight path.
- Level 2 adds a one-step ghost delay, requiring the player to anticipate the echo's position before stepping to avoid touching it.
- Level 3 increases the ghost delay to two steps, making the echo follow a longer path and harder to dodge while moving through the room.
- Level 4 adds a wall obstacle in the path, forcing the player to navigate around it while managing the echo's delayed position.
- Level 5 combines a longer ghost delay and the wall obstacle, making the rhythm matching and path navigation the hardest challenge.
