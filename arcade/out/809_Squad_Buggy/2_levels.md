NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base buggy speed and all three soldiers available, with simple open hills and few enemies, making the first control and combat flow easy.
- Level 2 raises the buggy speed to 130 and adds a mid-level obstacle wall, requiring tighter steering while soldiers still have full health for stable combat.
- Level 3 increases speed to 140 and bumps enemy movement, making enemy contact more frequent and requiring quicker soldier recovery during driving.
- Level 4 lowers lives to 2 and raises speed to 150 with denser hill obstacles, increasing bump frequency and making soldier recovery harder to maintain.
- Level 5 raises speed to 160, lowers lives to 1, and adds a tight canyon layout with frequent bumps, making the squad highly vulnerable and requiring perfect driving to hold the flag.
