NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed with three easy scoring targets, giving the player a steady start to learn the bumping controls.
- Level 2 increases the scroll speed and adds one more scoring target, requiring faster reaction timing while keeping the layout simple.
- Level 3 raises the speed and reduces lives to two, adding a rock target that requires precise jumps to avoid water hazards.
- Level 4 further increases speed and lowers lives to two, adding a second rock target that demands careful side-bumping to score without falling into water.
- Level 5 raises the speed to the highest setting and cuts lives to one, adding a final rock target with a water hazard to demand full precision from the player.
