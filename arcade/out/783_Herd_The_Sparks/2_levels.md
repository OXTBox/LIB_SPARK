NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base spark count and slow drift speed, giving the player steady control to guide sparks into the pen.
- Level 2 increases the spark count and drift speed, requiring faster movement to keep sparks inside the larger pen.
- Level 3 adds tighter arena constraints with faster drifting sparks, making fence contact more likely during navigation.
- Level 4 raises the spark count, drift speed, and pen size to demand precise, rapid control while avoiding the electric fence.
- Level 5 combines the highest spark count, fastest drift, and largest pen size for the final challenge of safely gathering all sparks.
