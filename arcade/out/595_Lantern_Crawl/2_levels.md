NOTES
- Level 1 uses the default 4 oil flasks and a slow 30 steps per second for easy navigation with ample refill timing.
- Level 2 reduces oil flask count to 3 and tightens the step speed slightly to 25, adding mild navigation pressure.
- Level 3 lowers the flask count to 3 and slows the step speed to 25, while the maze layout adds deeper dead-end branches requiring careful charge management.
- Level 4 reduces the oil flask count to 2 and slows the step speed to 20, forcing tight lantern charge timing across larger maze branches.
- Level 5 uses only 1 oil flask and the slowest step speed of 20, making lantern charge and dead-end refill timing the main challenge.
