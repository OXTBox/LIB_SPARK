NOTES
- Level 1 has no hazards and slow agents, giving the player a clear path to build the ball safely.
- Level 2 adds one drifting hazard and slightly faster agents, requiring the player to avoid the hazard while catching.
- Level 3 adds two hazards that drift closer to the agent paths, increasing the risk of accidental entanglement.
- Level 4 adds three hazards spread across the grid, forcing the player to navigate tighter routes between agents and hazards.
- Level 5 adds four hazards densely placed near the agent paths, making precise control required to complete the final quota.
