NOTES
- Level 1 has a flat, open tunnel path with few rocks above the dug cells, making digging and gem collection easy.
- Level 2 adds a small rock cluster above the main tunnel path, requiring the player to time rock falls while digging forward.
- Level 3 increases the rock count in the upper tunnel area, making rock falls more frequent and harder to time while maintaining passage.
- Level 4 reduces lives to 2 and raises the speed to 12, increasing the pressure on timing rock falls and tunnel passage.
- Level 5 reduces lives to 1 and raises the speed to 13, making precise timing of rock falls and tunnel passage nearly impossible.
