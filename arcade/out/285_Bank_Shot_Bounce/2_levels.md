NOTES
- Level 1 uses the basic table layout with three numbered balls in a straight row, giving the player a clear path to sink them in order with minimal risk.
- Level 2 adds one extra numbered ball to the row, requiring the player to maintain precise aim while avoiding the extra target to keep the sequence intact.
- Level 3 introduces a bumpper in the middle of the target row, which forces the player to time the shot so the ball bounces into the next numbered ball instead of missing it.
- Level 4 increases the number of numbered balls to six and adds a sliding pocket on the right rail, making the ball path unpredictable and raising the risk of missing the sequence.
- Level 5 adds seven numbered balls and reduces the player lives to one, with the felt slickness altered to slow the ball movement, demanding the most precise and powerful shots to complete the level.
