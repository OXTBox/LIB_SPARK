NOTES
- Level 1 only requires two switch presses to raise the red wall and lower the blue wall to reach the exit, giving a simple first test of the layout-changing mechanic.
- Level 2 adds a third colour switch, requiring three presses to raise all wall sections and clear the path to the exit.
- Level 3 increases the required press count to four by adding a second exit that needs a key from behind a raised wall section.
- Level 4 raises the required press count to five and tightens the path by placing the exit behind a wall section that must be lowered after the first two presses.
- Level 5 adds the final sixth press requirement and the tightest layout, where the exit is only reachable after lowering all wall sections in sequence.
