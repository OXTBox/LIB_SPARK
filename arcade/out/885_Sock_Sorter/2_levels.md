NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight room with three matching pairs placed in a direct line, giving clear movement guidance for first-time navigation.
- Level 2 widens the room and adds a secondary path for socks, requiring the player to plan routes between collection and placement zones without getting lost.
- Level 3 increases the number of pairs and places socks in scattered positions, adding more decision-making as the player must manage carrying limits while moving between distant collection and placement spots.
- Level 4 tightens the room layout and increases the pair count, forcing faster navigation while the player must carefully avoid placing unmatched socks on the laundry line.
- Level 5 uses the largest room with the maximum pair count and the shortest time limit, demanding precise coordination of carrying limits and matching placements under tight time pressure.
