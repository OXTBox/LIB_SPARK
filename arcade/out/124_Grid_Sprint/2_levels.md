NOTES
- Level 1 uses the slowest speed and the simplest straight road layout, giving the player full control over gear shifts and checkpoint timing.
- Level 2 adds a gentle curve that requires steady steering input while keeping the checkpoint timer recovery logic straightforward.
- Level 3 increases the curve steepness and adds more roadside sign placement, raising the risk of spinouts and life loss.
- Level 4 tightens the road turns and reduces lives to 2, making gear shift timing and avoiding signs the core challenge.
- Level 5 combines the tightest turns with the lowest lives, requiring precise gear management and quick checkpoint recovery to win.
