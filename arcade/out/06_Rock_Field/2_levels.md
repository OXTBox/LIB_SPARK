NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow big rocks and single splits to build a simple scoring path for the player.
- Level 2 adds more big rocks and slightly faster drift to require more careful navigation.
- Level 3 increases split counts so fragments create more collection opportunities and collision risk.
- Level 4 reduces lives and raises rock speed to demand faster split timing and precise movement.
- Level 5 combines the highest rock speed and most fragments to test full arcade survival and scoring.
