NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed and three fuel cans, with three lives, giving the player a simple start to learn the controls.
- Level 2 raises the speed to 130 and adds one more fuel can, while keeping three lives for a slightly more demanding but still manageable race.
- Level 3 increases speed to 140 and adds a second traffic car lane, making swerving and oil spot collisions more frequent.
- Level 4 raises speed to 150 and reduces lives to two, while adding more traffic lanes to increase collision risk.
- Level 5 sets the highest speed at 160 with seven fuel cans and two lives, requiring careful fuel management and constant traffic avoidance.
