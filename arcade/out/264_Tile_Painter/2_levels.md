NOTES
- Level 1 has no internal walls, so the player can paint every cell in a straight line and reach the exit quickly.
- Level 2 adds a single vertical wall column in the middle, forcing the player to navigate around it while painting the floor.
- Level 3 adds a second wall column offset from the first, creating a wider route the player must follow while changing colours.
- Level 4 adds a third wall column that narrows the available path, making colour changes and navigation harder to coordinate.
- Level 5 adds a fourth wall column that creates a tight, winding route, requiring careful colour changes and navigation to complete the floor before the timer ends.
