NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single row of blocks with a simple layout, giving the player plenty of time to aim and catch the ball.
- Level 2 adds a second row of blocks, requiring the player to clear blocks in two rows while the falling blocks move faster.
- Level 3 adds a third row of blocks, increasing the number of blocks to clear and the difficulty of timing the falling blocks.
- Level 4 adds a fourth row of blocks, making the layout denser and the timing of falling blocks much harder to manage.
- Level 5 adds a fifth row of blocks with the fastest falling speed, making precise aiming and timing required to clear all blocks and avoid landing on falling blocks or the ball.
