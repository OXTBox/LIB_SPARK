NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base 10 streaks and slow 120 speed, giving the player steady time to aim and fire without pressure.
- Level 2 adds 2 more streaks and keeps the slow speed, but the layout spreads the city blocks further apart to require more precise burst targeting.
- Level 3 raises the streak count to 14 and speeds up the fall to 130, making bursts arrive faster and harder to hit accurately.
- Level 4 increases both the streak count to 16 and the fall speed to 140, while expanding the burst radius to 55, adding more pressure on the player's aiming accuracy.
- Level 5 brings the maximum 18 streaks and the fastest 150 fall speed, with the largest 60 burst radius, creating the highest difficulty for the game.
