NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight open path for numbered tiles with minimal wall obstacles, making the counting sequence easy to follow.
- Level 2 adds scattered wall blocks that slightly bend the path, requiring more careful movement to stay in sequence.
- Level 3 introduces a tight corridor layout that forces the player to navigate between walls while collecting tiles in order.
- Level 4 increases wall density in the lower section of the layout, making the path tighter and harder to navigate under timed number swaps.
- Level 5 adds dense wall clusters across the entire layout, creating the most restrictive path and the highest difficulty for the counting challenge.
