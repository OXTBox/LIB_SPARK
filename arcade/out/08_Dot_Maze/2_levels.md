NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single chaser at slow speed with a short winding path, giving the player steady control to collect all dots.
- Level 2 adds a second chaser and a longer path, requiring the player to manage two chasing targets while moving through more dots.
- Level 3 raises chaser speed and adds more dots, making the chase faster and the path more congested to collect every dot.
- Level 4 introduces three chasers and higher speed, demanding precise movement to avoid all chasing targets while collecting the denser set of dots.
- Level 5 combines three fast chasers and the maximum number of dots, creating the tightest arcade challenge with the fastest chasing pressure.
