NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple open alley layout with slow dog speed and full lives to let the player learn basic parcel delivery.
- Level 2 narrows the central path with a faster dog speed to require quicker maze navigation without losing lives.
- Level 3 adds tighter maze walls and a faster dog speed to increase chase pressure while keeping the same parcel count.
- Level 4 reduces lives to 2 and raises dog speed further to demand faster reflexes during delivery.
- Level 5 raises dog speed to the highest level and cuts lives to 2 to create maximum clock pressure during delivery.
