NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest: only 2 dust cells and 2 vacuums, with wide open paths, so the player can sweep cleanly without much risk.
- Level 2 adds one more vacuum and a small furniture block that slightly narrows the path, making evasion harder while the dust accumulation rate stays low.
- Level 3 increases the vacuum count to 4 and adds more furniture blocks that block multiple paths, forcing the player to navigate tighter routes while managing dust.
- Level 4 raises the vacuum count to 5 and adds more furniture blocks that create complex dust accumulation zones, making balancing movement and sweeping much harder.
- Level 5 is the hardest: it has 6 vacuums and the most furniture blocks, creating the most complex dust accumulation zones and the tightest navigation paths, requiring careful balance of sweeping and evasion.
