NOTES
- Level 1 is easy: the ball moves slowly and the computer bat follows the ball with a simple steady pace, giving the player time to learn the arrow key control.
- Level 2 adds a small vertical obstacle between the bats, which forces the player to adjust their movement timing while the computer bat tracks the ball.
- Level 3 increases the ball speed and computer bat speed, making the rhythmic tracking harder to match with the player's arrow key inputs.
- Level 4 raises the bat height and speed further, requiring faster, more precise vertical movement from the player to keep the ball in play.
- Level 5 is the hardest: the highest ball speed and maximum bat height demand the most precise, rapid control from the player to score the required points before losing all lives.
