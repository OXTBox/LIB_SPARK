NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use wide, open layouts with generous timing windows, letting players focus on basic task coordination.
- Levels 3 to 5 narrow the yard space, shorten the timing windows, and reduce the drying seconds to force faster, more precise brush returns.
- The final level combines the tightest timing and smallest drying windows with the most planks, demanding constant coordination between both heroes.
