NOTES
- Level 1 has a single ship and the longest warning time, making the first steering control easy.
- Level 2 adds a second ship while shortening the warning time, requiring faster beam adjustments.
- Level 3 increases the ship count and raises the ship speed, making precise beam tracking harder.
- Level 4 packs four ships at once with the shortest warning time, demanding quick coordinated beam control.
- Level 5 keeps four ships but raises the ship speed to the maximum, creating the most difficult beam balancing challenge.
