- Level 1 uses a simple straight path with three pins in a short sequence to teach basic pin raising.
- Level 2 adds a small detour before the pins, requiring the player to plan movement before raising the first two pins.
- Level 3 widens the room layout and adds more pins, increasing the sequencing complexity of the required order.
- Level 4 introduces longer detours between pins, demanding careful route planning to raise the pins in the correct sequence.
- Level 5 packs nine pins across a wider room with all hints visible, maximizing sequencing difficulty and requiring precise movement planning.
