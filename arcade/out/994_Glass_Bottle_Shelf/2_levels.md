LEVELS
level | name | bottles per minute (6 to 24) | shelves (1 to 3) | cracks allowed (5 down to 2)
1 | First Catch | 6 | 1 | 5

3 | Two Shelves | 12 | 2 | 3
4 | Quick Rolls | 15 | 2 | 2
5 | Tight Timing | 20 | 3 | 2
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single shelf with slow rolling bottles and generous cracks to build basic timing control.
- Level 2 raises the bottle roll speed and reduces allowed cracks to require faster basket movement.
- Level 3 adds a second shelf to increase the number of bottles to catch while maintaining tighter timing.
- Level 4 raises the bottle roll speed and cuts allowed cracks to demand precise basket positioning.
- Level 5 combines three shelves, the highest bottle roll speed, and the lowest allowed cracks for maximum timing difficulty.
