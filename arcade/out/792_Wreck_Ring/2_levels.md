NOTES
- Level 1 is easy: the arena has a single open path with no mines, so the player can collect all power-ups without health risk.
- Level 2 adds a lava pit section that forces the player to steer carefully to avoid damage while still reaching the end.
- Level 3 increases the number of cars and adds more ramps, requiring faster and more precise steering to stay ahead of competitors.
- Level 4 introduces more mine placements that reduce health, forcing the player to time power-up collection and precise driving.
- Level 5 is the hardest: it combines the most cars, the highest speed, and dense mine layouts that demand constant careful steering to win the round.
