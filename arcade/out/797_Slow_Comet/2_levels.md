NOTES
- Level 1 has few comets and a light tail push for a relaxed first playthrough.
- Level 2 adds one more comet and a stronger tail push to require more careful vertical dodging.
- Level 3 increases comet count and tail push intensity to narrow the safe vertical space.
- Level 4 raises comet density and tail push to make precise vertical control harder.
- Level 5 has the maximum comet count and full tail push for the most challenging final level.
