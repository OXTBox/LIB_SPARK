NOTES
- Level 1 has no guards and a short lock distance, giving the player a comfortable start to learn the swap mechanic.
- Level 2 adds one guard walking between the locks while keeping the swap window wide for easier timing practice.
- Level 3 increases the guard count and shrinks the swap window, requiring faster timing as guards block the locks more often.
- Level 4 raises the guard count and tightens the swap window to the minimum, making the timing challenge very difficult.
- Level 5 packs the maximum guards and the shortest swap window, creating the hardest timing pressure for the player.
