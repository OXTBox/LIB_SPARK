NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed and a simple lane layout with few static walls, letting the player focus on basic lane movement and cassette collection.
- Level 2 increases the speed slightly and adds more static walls, requiring the player to manage lane shifts more carefully while still collecting data.
- Level 3 introduces more chrome pillars in the lanes, raising the risk of losing lives from falling obstacles.
- Level 4 raises the speed further and densifies both static walls and chrome pillars, demanding faster lane transitions and more careful obstacle avoidance.
- Level 5 is the hardest with the highest speed, the most obstacles, and reduced lives, testing full lane control and obstacle dodging under high pressure.
