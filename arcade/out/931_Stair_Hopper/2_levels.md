NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base water speed and only one drifting ledge, giving full control over timing for the first climb.
- Level 2 increases the water speed and adds a second drifting ledge, requiring faster reaction to avoid the rising tide.
- Level 3 raises the water speed further and adds a third drifting ledge that moves faster, demanding precise timing for every gap jump.
- Level 4 raises the water speed to the highest level value and adds a fourth drifting ledge, making the tower climb much harder to survive.
- Level 5 raises the water speed to the maximum value and adds a fifth drifting ledge with the fastest drift, requiring perfect timing to reach the top before the water reaches the bottom.
