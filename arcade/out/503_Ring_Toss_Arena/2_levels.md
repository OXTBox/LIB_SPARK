NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base post speed and the full ring count, giving a relaxed first attempt at the core throw-and-collide mechanic.
- Level 2 increases post speed and reduces the ring count, forcing quicker throws to stay within the play area.
- Level 3 raises post speed further and adds a third sliding post, increasing collision difficulty.
- Level 4 slows post movement but adds a second ring count, requiring more precise throws to hit all targets.
- Level 5 sets the highest post speed with only one ring left, demanding maximum accuracy for the final win condition.
