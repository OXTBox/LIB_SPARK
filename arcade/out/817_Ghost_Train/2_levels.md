NOTES
- Level 1 has fewer bends and a high tip limit, giving the player simple left-right movement to practice the cart controls.
- Level 2 adds more bends and slightly faster movement, requiring more careful lean decisions to stay balanced.
- Level 3 increases bend density and speed while lowering the tip limit, making the balancing challenge much harder.
- Level 4 keeps high bend density but adds sharper wire angles, raising the difficulty of avoiding tips during sharp turns.
- Level 5 combines maximum bend density with the lowest tip limit and fastest speed, making the hardest balancing and ghost sign avoidance challenge.
