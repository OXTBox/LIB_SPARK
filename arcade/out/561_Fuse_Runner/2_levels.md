NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use slow fuse speeds and wider spacing to let the player learn the switch timing without pressure.
- Levels 3 to 5 increase fuse speed and switch density, forcing faster reaction and tighter timing against the burning wire.
- Level 5 combines the highest fuse speed and most switches to demand precise coordination across the entire corridor.
