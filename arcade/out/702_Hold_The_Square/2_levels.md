NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base square speed and four drones, giving a slow, relaxed start to learn movement and behind-drone movement.
- Level 2 raises the square speed slightly while keeping four drones, requiring faster positioning while still balancing score fill and drone evasion.
- Level 3 adds a fifth drone and raises the square speed further, creating tighter timing between square movement and drone entry to survive.
- Level 4 reduces lives to two and raises the square speed more, making drone evasion and score filling much harder to coordinate.
- Level 5 raises the square speed to the highest setting and adds six drones, requiring constant precise positioning to fill the score bar without losing all lives.
