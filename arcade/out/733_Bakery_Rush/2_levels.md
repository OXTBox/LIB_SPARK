NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single oven with the longest bake time and one customer, giving the player ample time to practice the basic carry movement.
- Level 2 adds a second oven and a shorter bake time, requiring the player to manage multiple bread sources while moving faster.
- Level 3 increases the number of ovens and customers, raising the pressure of timing deliveries against the shorter bake window.
- Level 4 expands the oven count and customer density, demanding faster reflexes and tighter timing as the bake time shrinks further.
- Level 5 combines all four ovens and the maximum customer count with the shortest bake time, creating the highest level of timing pressure.
