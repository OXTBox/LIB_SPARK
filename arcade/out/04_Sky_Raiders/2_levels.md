NOTES
- Level 1 has no speed scaling and no shields, so clearing the rows is straightforward with steady cannon movement.
- Level 2 raises the raider speed by 5 world units, making shot timing harder as rows advance faster.
- Level 3 adds a 30 percent shot chance, requiring the cannon to fire before raiders step down at the edges.
- Level 4 raises the shot chance to 50 percent and adds one shield, adding defensive complexity to the timing.
- Level 5 raises the speed to 30 world units, increases the shot chance to 70 percent, and adds two shields, making full row clearance the hardest challenge.
