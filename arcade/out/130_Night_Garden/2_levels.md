NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple open hedge layout with a single gardener path, giving easy flower collection and stable movement.
- Level 2 adds internal dead-end branches inside the hedge maze, increasing path-following complexity for the player.
- Level 3 introduces a closed-loop hedge path with more flowers clustered in dead ends, raising the challenge of finding all glowing flowers.
- Level 4 adds a lantern light zone near the gate, requiring the player to stand in the warm light to return to the gate after collecting flowers.
- Level 5 combines the full maze layout with a tight gardener path and clustered flowers in dead ends, making the path-following and lantern interaction the hardest challenge.
