NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use few nests and a wide hatch window, making the first chicks easy to guide.
- Level 3 adds a wider door, which makes chicks wander outside more often and costs more lives.
- Levels 4 and 5 raise the nest count and shrink the hatch window, requiring faster navigation and more careful guiding.
- Level 5 combines the highest nest count with the narrowest door and shortest hatch time for the hardest challenge.
