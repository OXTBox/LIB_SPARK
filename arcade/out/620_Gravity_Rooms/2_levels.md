NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use wide, open rooms with few spikes and no moving spikes, giving simple gravity flipping practice.
- Levels 3 to 5 add tighter room boundaries, more spike cells, and moving spikes that require careful timing to avoid.
- Level 5 combines the most complex layout with the highest spike and moving spike counts, making it the hardest level.
