NOTES
- Level 1 uses the slowest ball speed and the fewest balls, giving the player plenty of time to learn the climb and step over the rolling balls safely.
- Level 2 raises the ball speed and adds one more ball, making the timing of stepping over the ramp faster and harder to manage.
- Level 3 increases the ball speed further and adds another ball, requiring faster decisions while the ramp geometry stays the same but the timing becomes tighter.
- Level 4 raises the ball speed again and reduces the player lives to 2, so a fall or missed ball costs more, making the climb much harder.
- Level 5 raises the ball speed to the highest value and adds the final ball, with only 2 lives left, making the climb the most difficult of all levels.
