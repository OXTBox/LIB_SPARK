NOTES
- Level 1 has a single lift and one waiting person, with simple straight corridors; the player only needs to ride the lift once to reach the top.
- Level 2 adds a second waiting person and a second lift, requiring the player to ride multiple lifts to carry everyone to the top.
- Level 3 introduces a third lift and a longer corridor, with more waiting people that must be carried before the player can advance.
- Level 4 adds a fourth lift and a more complex layout with multiple waiting people across floors, increasing the number of lifts the player must navigate.
- Level 5 is the hardest, with five lifts and a dense layout of waiting people across all floors, requiring careful navigation of every lift and corridor to reach the top.
