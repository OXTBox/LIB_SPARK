NOTES
- Level 1 has a single slow whirlwind and few gems, giving the player a relaxed first chase.
- Level 2 adds a second whirlwind and more gems, increasing the chase pressure slightly.
- Level 3 raises the whirlwind count and speed, making the path unpredictable and faster.
- Level 4 adds more whirlwinds and faster movement, forcing the player to act quickly to collect gems.
- Level 5 packs the maximum whirlwinds and speed, creating the most challenging edge-detection and chase risk.
