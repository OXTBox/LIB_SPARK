NOTES
- Level 1 uses the simplest 3-beacon layout with the full 3 lives, giving a forgiving first playthrough.
- Level 2 adds one beacon to the layout while keeping the same speed and lives, requiring the player to navigate further to light the extra target.
- Level 3 increases the beacon count to 5 and reduces lives to 2, raising the difficulty of completing the level without losing a life.
- Level 4 expands the beacon count to 6 and keeps lives at 2, forcing more careful navigation to light all targets before the level ends.
- Level 5 adds the final beacon to 7 and reduces lives to 1, making the level the most challenging as the player must complete the maze without any remaining lives.
