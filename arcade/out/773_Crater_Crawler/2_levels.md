NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed with three boulders and no saucer bombs, giving the player a relaxed first run through the course.
- Level 2 adds one saucer that drops bombs, increasing the hazard count and requiring the player to manage jumps against new threats.
- Level 3 raises the hazard count to five with two saucers, making the real-time bomb and crater generation more frequent and harder to navigate.
- Level 4 increases the speed to 130 and lowers the lives to two, demanding faster reaction and more careful hazard avoidance.
- Level 5 raises the speed to 140, keeps the highest hazard count of seven, and keeps only two lives, making the course the most difficult of the five.
