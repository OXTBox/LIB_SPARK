NOTES
- Level 1 uses 1 drone at slow speed and full air time, giving the player ample time to practice the jump arc and platform timing.
- Level 2 adds a second drone and slightly faster patrol speed, increasing the pressure on the player's dodging reflexes.
- Level 3 increases the drone count to 3 and raises drone speed, while reducing air time to force faster descent.
- Level 4 adds a fourth drone with faster patrol speed and cuts air time further, making the vertical path more challenging.
- Level 5 introduces 5 fast patrol drones and the shortest air supply, requiring precise timing to reach the exit before air runs out.
