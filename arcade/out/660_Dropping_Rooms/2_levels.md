NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base hole speed and smoke seconds, giving the player a steady, forgiving start to learn the vertical hole movement.
- Level 2 adds one more room to the tower, extending the chase time and increasing the pressure on timing the hole passes.
- Level 3 keeps the base speeds but stacks five rooms, making the synchronized hole and smoke movement harder to manage across the full tower.
- Level 4 raises the hole speed and lowers the smoke seconds, forcing faster reaction timing while leaving less room for error.
- Level 5 combines the fastest hole speed and shortest smoke seconds, making the vertical chase the most tense and difficult level.
