NOTES
- Level 1 has a slow light beam and wide open space, giving the player enough time to navigate and collect all coins safely.
- Level 2 increases the light speed slightly and adds a narrower central path, making the timing of shadow shifts harder to manage.
- Level 3 introduces pillars that cast protective shadows, but the beam moves faster and the safe zones are smaller, raising the risk of being tagged.
- Level 4 raises the light speed further and tightens the courtyard layout, reducing the available safe space and demanding precise timing.
- Level 5 is the final level with the highest light speed and the most compact layout, requiring exact coordination of movement and shadow timing to collect all coins and reach the exit.
