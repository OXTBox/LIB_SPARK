LEVELS
level | name | toys (4 to 12) | room size cells (6 to 12) | cat seconds (30 down to 10)

NOTES
- Level 1 uses a small room with few toys and a slow cat to teach basic pushing.
- Level 2 adds more toys and a slightly faster cat to increase push difficulty.
- Level 3 expands the room and toy count while the cat moves faster to create more knock-out risks.
- Level 4 raises the toy count and slows the cat to force precise pushing without losing lives.
- Level 5 combines the largest room, most toys, and the fastest cat for the hardest challenge.
