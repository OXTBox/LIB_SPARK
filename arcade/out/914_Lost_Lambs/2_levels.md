NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 add lambs and one wolf, increasing the number of lambs to guard while the maze stays open.
- Levels 3 to 5 shrink the maze cells, tighten the space, and raise the wolf count to force faster, more careful navigation.
- Level 5 combines the most lambs, the highest wolf count, and the smallest maze size for the hardest challenge.
