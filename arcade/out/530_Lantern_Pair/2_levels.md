NOTES
- Level 1 is a simple straight corridor with one open column for the lantern to travel, giving the player full control over the lantern's path with minimal obstacles.
- Level 2 adds a second vertical wall column inside the corridor, forcing the player to navigate around the wall while still guiding the lantern through the single open column.
- Level 3 expands the number of wall columns inside the corridor, increasing the maze complexity and making the lantern's path require more careful swapping between the hero and the player.
- Level 4 adds a third wall column, further narrowing the open path and demanding precise coordination between movement and lantern swapping to stay within the move limit.
- Level 5 adds a fourth wall column, creating the tightest maze layout of the game with the smallest possible path for the lantern, requiring maximum precision in movement and swapping to reach the exit within the move limit.
