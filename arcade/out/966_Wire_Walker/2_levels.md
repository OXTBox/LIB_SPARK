NOTES
- Level 1 uses a short wire with slow gusts and low strength so the player can focus on basic forward movement and lean control.
- Level 2 increases the wire length and gust frequency while raising gust strength to add more wind challenge.
- Level 3 further increases gust frequency and strength while extending the wire length to require sustained balance management.
- Level 4 raises gust frequency and strength to create faster, more intense wind shifts while keeping the wire length moderate.
- Level 5 combines the maximum wire length with the highest gust frequency and strength to demand precise real-time balance control.
