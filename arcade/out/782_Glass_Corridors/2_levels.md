NOTES
- Level 1 is the simplest route: the player moves straight to the central crystal with only one glass wall section to navigate.
- Level 2 adds one one-way door near the path, requiring the player to press Space to open it before moving through.
- Level 3 increases the maze complexity with more one-way doors scattered along the route, forcing careful timing of door activation.
- Level 4 and 5 add tighter glass wall sections and more one-way doors, making the hidden route harder to find and requiring faster reflexes.
