NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight open corridor with no internal walls, so the player can reach every tile quickly and match crystals in a simple row.
- Level 2 adds a single internal wall that forces the player to backtrack between two sections, increasing the number of steps needed to clear the room.
- Level 3 adds a second internal wall forming a narrow corridor, which forces longer backtracking and makes matching crystals in separate rows harder.
- Level 4 increases the wall density, creating a maze-like layout that requires careful navigation and multiple backtracks to reach all tiles.
- Level 5 combines the highest wall density with a layout that forces the player to traverse the room in a complex path, maximizing steps while still remaining winnable.
