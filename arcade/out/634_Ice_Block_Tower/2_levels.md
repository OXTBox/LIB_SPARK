NOTES
- Level 1 uses a low target height and slow slide speed to let players learn basic stacking without collapse risk.
- Level 2 raises the target height and increases slide speed, requiring faster stacking timing to stay safe.
- Level 3 raises the target height and speeds up slide motion, making melting collapse risk harder to manage.
- Level 4 raises the target height and increases slide speed while shortening melt time, demanding precise balance of stacking and stability.
- Level 5 sets the highest target height with the fastest slide speed and shortest melt time, requiring expert timing to complete safely.
