NOTES
- Levels 1 to 2 add more stops while the bus moves faster, making it harder to slow for each passenger.
- Levels 3 to 4 increase the stop count and reduce the lamp time, so catching passengers becomes more difficult.
- Level 5 has the most stops and the shortest lamp time, requiring precise slowing to catch all passengers without missing any.
