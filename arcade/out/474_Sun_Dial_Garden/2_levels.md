NOTES
- Level 1 uses a slow shadow sweep and few flowers to let the player learn the safe shadow zones.
- Level 2 adds more flowers and a faster shadow to require quicker movement between safe zones.
- Level 3 increases the shadow speed and sand density to make the safe zones harder to track.
- Level 4 raises the flower count and shadow speed to demand steady, fast navigation through narrow safe zones.
- Level 5 uses the maximum shadow speed and highest flower count to test full control of the sweeping shadow pattern.
