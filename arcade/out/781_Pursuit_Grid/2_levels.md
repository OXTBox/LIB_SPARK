NOTES
- Level 1 and 2 use slow hunters with wide open paths, giving the player a steady chance to collect all five keys.
- Level 3 adds one hunter speed increase every fourth turn, making hunters faster and harder to avoid.
- Level 4 increases hunter speed further, forcing the player to plan key collection before hunters close in.
- Level 5 adds the final speed increase, with hunters moving faster and tighter paths, making the level the most difficult.
