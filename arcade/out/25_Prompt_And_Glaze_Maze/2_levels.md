NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow enemies and fewer powerups to teach basic movement and dot collection.
- Level 2 adds one more enemy and slightly faster chase speed to require smoother dodging.
- Level 3 introduces more corners in the path to increase polyhedron bounce complexity.
- Level 4 raises enemy count and speed to demand frequent powerup use for charm or prompt.
- Level 5 adds the most enemies and fastest chase speed to require constant agent switching for survival.
