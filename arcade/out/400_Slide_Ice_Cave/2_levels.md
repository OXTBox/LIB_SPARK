NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed and the simplest obstacle layout, giving smooth sliding with only basic stops.
- Level 2 adds one more rock to the layout, requiring players to navigate more obstacles while sliding.
- Level 3 reduces the player speed to 100, making sliding less smooth and harder to control.
- Level 4 increases the obstacle count to 5 and lowers the speed further to 80, making precise stops much harder.
- Level 5 uses the lowest speed of 60 with the highest obstacle count of 6, making the level the most difficult of the set.
