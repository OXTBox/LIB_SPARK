NOTES
- Level 1 uses the slowest falling speed and the most lives, giving the player time to map the room and collect all gems safely.
- Level 2 raises the falling speed slightly and keeps the same layout, making the constant pressure of disappearing cells harder to manage.
- Level 3 increases the speed further and reduces the lives to 2, forcing the player to prioritize gem collection before the room collapses.
- Level 4 raises the speed again and lowers the lives to 1, so the player must act quickly and avoid unsafe zones to survive the level.
- Level 5 uses the highest speed and the lowest lives, requiring the player to navigate fast-moving cells and reach the exit before the floor fully collapses.
