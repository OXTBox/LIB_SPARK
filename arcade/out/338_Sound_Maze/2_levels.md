NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight maze with clear wall traces and single-floor layout, making navigation easy with spatial memory.
- Level 2 adds faint wall flash cues on touched walls, slightly increasing the challenge of recalling the maze layout.
- Level 3 introduces a second floor connected by a green ramp, requiring players to remember the floor connection to navigate the wider maze.
- Level 4 reduces visible maze area by narrowing wall spacing, making the maze harder to recall without visual outlines.
- Level 5 combines reduced visible area, a second floor ramp, and tighter wall spacing, making the maze the most difficult to navigate by memory.
