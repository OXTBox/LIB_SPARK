NOTES
- Level 1 is the simplest: one fire at the house, no new fires start, and the player can reach the house with a single trip without any risk.
- Level 2 adds two fires at the start and a shorter new fire timer, so the player must move faster to reach the house before new fires spread.
- Level 3 adds a fire switch that starts new fires when the player walks into it, adding a coordination challenge to the water transport.
- Level 4 shortens the new fire timer further, making the spread faster and requiring the player to complete trips before fires reach the well.
- Level 5 tightens the new fire timer to the shortest value, forcing the player to manage every trip carefully to survive the spreading fires.
