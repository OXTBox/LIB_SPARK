NOTES
- Level 1 uses a slow, low-density swarm with minimal splitting to let the player learn basic shooting and spinning controls.
- Level 2 increases blob count and speed, requiring faster spin and more precise bullet targeting.
- Level 3 raises both swarm density and speed, making collision survival harder while splitting creates denser clusters.
- Level 4 adds more blobs at higher speed, demanding quicker reactions and more efficient shooting to avoid losing lives.
- Level 5 combines maximum density, highest speed, and maximum splitting to test full survival and swarm management skills.
