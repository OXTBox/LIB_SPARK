NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight main track with two short sidings, giving the player clear timing to learn the steering mechanic.
- Level 2 adds a second main track with three sidings, requiring the player to manage multiple tracks and switch states at once.
- Level 3 expands the layout with four sidings and five trains, increasing the complexity of switch timing and collision risk.
- Level 4 introduces tighter track spacing and faster train movement, making precise switch navigation much harder to execute.
- Level 5 packs all five sidings into a dense layout with eight trains and the fastest train speed, demanding maximum timing and collision avoidance skill.
