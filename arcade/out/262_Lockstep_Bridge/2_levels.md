NOTES
- Level 1 uses two static gaps on a short bridge, giving the player a clear starting rhythm to learn the synchronized crossing.
- Level 2 adds a third static gap and a slightly longer bridge, requiring the player to maintain sync across more segments without moving gaps.
- Level 3 introduces the first timing challenge by adding four static gaps with wider spacing, demanding precise alignment between the two bridges.
- Level 4 adds one moving gap that shifts position, requiring the player to adjust timing while keeping both heroes synchronized.
- Level 5 combines six static gaps and two moving gaps on the longest bridge, demanding perfect continuous synchronization across the full length.
