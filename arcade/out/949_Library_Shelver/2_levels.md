NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight corridor with four shelves and slow reader requests to build basic navigation and placement.
- Level 2 adds a short turn and five shelves, requiring the player to navigate corners while placing more books in sequence.
- Level 3 introduces a branching layout with six shelves and tighter request timing, increasing the difficulty of finding correct shelves quickly.
- Level 4 expands the layout with seven shelves and more books, demanding precise navigation and careful ordering of placements.
- Level 5 features the full eight-shelf layout with the fastest request timing, making sequence matching and quick shelf navigation the hardest challenge.
