NOTES
- Level 1 has few gutters and slow overflow, giving the barrel plenty of time to align without missing rain.
- Level 2 adds one more gutter and slightly faster overflow, requiring quicker barrel movement to stay aligned.
- Level 3 increases gutter count and shortens overflow time, making precise alignment harder to maintain.
- Level 4 raises the gutter count and cuts overflow time further, demanding faster barrel steering to catch all rain.
- Level 5 has the most gutters and the fastest overflow, making it the hardest level to keep the barrel aligned and catch all rain.
