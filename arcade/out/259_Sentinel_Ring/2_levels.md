NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest: the sentinel turns slowly and only one pulse fires at a time, so the player can circle the ring edge safely.
- Level 2 adds a second pulse at once and a faster sentinel turn speed, forcing the player to circle faster while dodging two pulses.
- Level 3 increases the pulse count to three and raises the sentinel turn speed, creating tighter windows to collect pickups without being hit.
- Level 4 raises the pulse count to four and further increases the sentinel turn speed, making the dodging and collection rhythm much harder to maintain.
- Level 5 is the hardest: five pulses fire at once with the fastest sentinel turn speed, requiring constant fast circling to stay alive and collect all pickups.
