NOTES
- Level 1 uses the simplest maze layout with 3 survivors and standard speed for a relaxed first playthrough.
- Level 2 adds a single internal wall segment to restrict movement while keeping the same survivor count and speed.
- Level 3 increases the survivor count to 4 and raises the speed to 100 for tighter navigation challenges.
- Level 4 adds two internal wall segments and reduces lives to 2 to demand faster pinging and more careful path selection.
- Level 5 adds a third internal wall segment, raises speed to 60, and increases survivors to 5 for the hardest level.
