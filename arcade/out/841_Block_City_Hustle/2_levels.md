NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed and three phone booth jobs with minimal patrol cars, giving a clear start to learn movement and car use.
- Level 2 adds one more phone booth job and slightly higher speed, requiring faster navigation through the city layout.
- Level 3 raises the speed and adds two more phone booth jobs, while the layout becomes more congested with more street paths to navigate.
- Level 4 lowers the lives to two and increases patrol car pressure, requiring careful heat management to avoid losing lives.
- Level 5 raises the speed to the highest value, adds the final phone booth job, and keeps the lowest lives, making the dynamic chase and heat interactions the hardest to manage.
