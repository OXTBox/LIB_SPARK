NOTES
- Level 1 is the simplest maze: straight mirrored paths with no internal walls, so the twin follows the player's steps directly to the exit.
- Level 2 adds a small internal wall block that forces the player to navigate around it before the twin mirrors the path, increasing step complexity.
- Level 3 adds a second internal wall cluster that creates more mirrored branches, requiring the player to choose a longer path to keep the twin's route consistent.
- Level 4 adds a wall that blocks the direct mirrored path, forcing the player to take a longer detour, and the maze becomes tighter with fewer open cells.
- Level 5 combines all previous wall clusters into a dense maze layout, making the mirrored twin's path highly complex and requiring precise timing to match the exit pad.
