NOTES
- Level 1 uses two straight mirrors with no turnable mirrors, so the beam travels straight and the player only needs to walk to the target.
- Level 2 adds one turnable mirror, so the player must angle the beam correctly to avoid hitting a wall while reaching the target.
- Level 3 adds more mirrors and two turnable mirrors, requiring precise reflection planning to hit the target without missing the beam path.
- Level 4 adds three turnable mirrors, making the reflection logic more complex and the beam path harder to plan accurately.
- Level 5 adds four turnable mirrors, the most complex layout, requiring careful spatial planning to hit the target without hitting walls.
