NOTES
- Level 1 has a single open corridor with no internal walls, giving the player full movement freedom and the easiest path to the final arena size.
- Level 2 adds one internal wall in the middle of the open space, forcing the player to navigate around it for every movement step.
- Level 3 adds a second internal wall, creating more branching paths and requiring more careful turns to avoid blocked movement routes.
- Level 4 introduces a partial wall row that restricts horizontal movement, forcing the player to alternate between vertical and horizontal moves more frequently.
- Level 5 adds a full wall row with multiple internal walls, creating tight corridors that demand precise movement and speed boost usage to survive the shrinking arena.
