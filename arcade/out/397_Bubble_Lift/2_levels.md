NOTES
- Level 1 has no eels and a slow bubble speed, giving the player full time to learn the ride mechanic.
- Level 2 adds one slow eel and one more ledge, requiring the player to time bubble rides while avoiding early threats.
- Level 3 increases ledges and adds two eels, making bubble timing and eel avoidance harder to coordinate.
- Level 4 adds three eels and more ledges, raising the difficulty of avoiding fast-moving threats during rides.
- Level 5 has the most ledges and four fast eels, demanding precise coordination of movement and bubble timing for the hardest challenge.
