NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest start: no junk rows, slow drops, and a simple four-block clear target to learn the controls.
- Level 2 adds one junk row and a faster drop speed, requiring you to navigate the falling shape around existing well blocks without missing the clear target.
- Level 3 increases both drop speed and junk rows, making precise sliding and timing harder as the well fills faster.
- Level 4 raises drop speed and adds more junk rows, demanding faster reaction and careful alignment to clear rows without losing lives.
- Level 5 is the hardest: the fastest drop speed and the most junk rows require constant precise control to clear rows before the well fills to the top.
