NOTES
- Level 1 uses wide, evenly spaced spotlights with a single actor for simple movement and clear scoring.
- Level 2 adds a second actor and slightly shorter cues to require more careful navigation between lights.
- Level 3 increases the number of spotlights and adds more actors to narrow the safe paths between cues.
- Level 4 tightens the spotlight spacing and shortens the cues further to demand faster, precise movement.
- Level 5 packs eight close spotlights with five actors and the shortest cue time to test full precision and life management.
