NOTES
- Level 1 uses few scarecrows and slow crows to let players learn the guarding mechanic.
- Level 2 adds one scarecrow and faster crows, requiring quicker movement to protect crops.
- Level 3 increases crop density and crow speed, making guarding harder as more cells need protection.
- Level 4 raises scarecrow count and crow speed, demanding precise positioning of all scarecrows.
- Level 5 combines maximum scarecrows and highest crow speed, making safe field navigation the hardest challenge.
