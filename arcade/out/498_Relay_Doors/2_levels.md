NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple open corridor with one door and three plates, giving the player a short, straightforward first test of plate swapping and door opening.
- Level 2 adds a second door and four plates, requiring the player to swap plates between two separate door zones to open both doors before moving forward.
- Level 3 expands the maze to 14x14 with five plates and three doors, adding a mid maze wall section that forces the player to coordinate plate swaps across multiple door clusters.
- Level 4 increases the maze size and door count to four doors, with more plates to manage, requiring longer coordination between swaps and door openings before reaching the exit.
- Level 5 is the hardest level with a 16x16 maze, seven plates and five doors, demanding precise, repeated plate swapping across multiple door clusters to open all doors and reach the exit together.
