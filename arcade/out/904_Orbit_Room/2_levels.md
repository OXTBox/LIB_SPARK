NOTES
- Level 1 has a single rotating ring and few sparks, so the wall rhythm is slow and the timing challenge is gentle.
- Level 2 adds a second rotating ring and more sparks, making the gap alignment harder to time consistently.
- Level 3 increases the ring count to three and reduces lives to two, raising the pressure of missing a step.
- Level 4 adds a fourth rotating ring and keeps lives at two, requiring tighter coordination of the wall rhythm and spark avoidance.
- Level 5 adds a fifth rotating ring with only one life left, making the final gap alignment the most demanding challenge.
