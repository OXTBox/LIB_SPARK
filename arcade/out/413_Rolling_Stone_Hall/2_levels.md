NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base stone count and slowest ball speed, giving time to learn the alcove and door timing.
- Level 2 adds one more stone ball and a wider hall, increasing the pressure from rolling stones.
- Level 3 raises the ball speed and adds a third alcove, requiring faster timing while managing more stones.
- Level 4 increases the hall length and ball speed further, making stone positions harder to predict.
- Level 5 combines the highest stone count, fastest ball speed, and most alcoves, demanding full timing and planning control.
