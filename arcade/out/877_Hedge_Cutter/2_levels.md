LEVELS
level | name | maze size cells (8 to 15) | regrow seconds (60 down to 20) | par cuts (5 to 20)

4 | Cramped Garden | 15 | 30 | 12
5 | Overgrown Maze | 15 | 20 | 15
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a wide open maze with a long regrow delay, giving the player easy room to cut hedges without them blocking the path.
- Level 2 adds tighter maze spacing and a shorter regrow delay, forcing the player to cut hedges faster to keep the path clear.
- Level 3 introduces more winding maze layout and a tighter cut limit, raising the difficulty of managing the growing hedge.
- Level 4 narrows the maze space and lowers the regrow delay further, making the path harder to maintain while the hedge regrows quickly.
- Level 5 combines the smallest regrow delay with the highest cut limit and the largest maze, creating the hardest challenge of the game.
