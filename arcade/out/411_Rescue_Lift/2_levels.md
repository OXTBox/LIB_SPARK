NOTES
- Level 1 is easy because the cage speed is slow and only three miners wait on the first ledges, so the player can reach every miner without rushing.
- Level 2 adds one more miner to the ledges, requiring the player to move the cage through more ledges before finishing the level.
- Level 3 increases the number of falling rocks, making it harder to time cage movements without cracking the cage.
- Level 4 adds more miners and tighter ledge spacing, forcing the player to balance cage capacity and timing more carefully.
- Level 5 is the hardest because the shaft is deepest, there are the most miners, and only one life remains to survive the falling rocks and cracked cage risks.
