NOTES
- Level 1 has 3 floors and a slow rotation speed, giving the player enough time to follow the stairs to the top without pressure.
- Level 2 adds one floor and increases the rotation speed, requiring faster reaction to the shifting doors.
- Level 3 adds another floor and raises the rotation speed further, making the shifting doors harder to track while climbing.
- Level 4 reduces lives to 2 and raises the rotation speed even more, demanding quicker navigation to avoid losing lives.
- Level 5 adds the final floor, lowers lives to 2, and uses the highest rotation speed, making the tower traps the most difficult to avoid.
