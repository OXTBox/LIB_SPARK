LEVELS
level | name | rows (3 to 8) | birds (1 to 5) | safe seconds (6 down to 2)
1 | Green Field Start | 3 | 1 | 6
2 | Misty Row Path | 4 | 2 | 5
3 | Dense Field Loop | 5 | 3 | 4
4 | Tight Timing Zone | 6 | 4 | 3
5 | Fast Bird Chase | 8 | 5 | 2
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a short row path with one bird and a long safe window for easy practice.
- Level 2 widens the row layout and adds a second bird to increase timing pressure.
- Level 3 increases the number of rows and birds, narrowing the safe window for faster play.
- Level 4 tightens the safe window and adds more birds to demand quick seed sowing.
- Level 5 uses the full row count with the fewest safe seconds and the most birds for the hardest challenge.
