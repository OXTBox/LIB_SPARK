NOTES
- Level 1 uses short straight paths with one door per side and three plates, giving the player quick practice with basic door opening and control swapping.
- Level 2 adds a mid maze wall that forces heroes to cross separate corridors, increasing the need to coordinate movement between the two heroes.
- Level 3 lengthens the routes and adds a second wall section that forces more careful timing while doors close on a timer.
- Level 4 adds a tighter corridor layout with more door placements, increasing pressure on door timing and split control coordination.
- Level 5 combines the longest routes, most door placements and tightest corridors to require precise timing and coordinated movement from both heroes.
