NOTES
- Level 1 uses a short tune with wide spacing to let the player learn the bell movement and basic ring sequence.
- Level 2 adds one more bell to the sequence and tightens the note spacing to require faster movement through the ropes.
- Level 3 increases the tune length and narrows the rope path, making precise movement harder while keeping the core ring rules unchanged.
- Level 4 expands the bell count and further reduces note spacing, demanding faster navigation and accurate sequence matching.
- Level 5 combines the longest tune and tightest spacing with the most bells, testing full rhythm coordination and precise rope movement under pressure.
