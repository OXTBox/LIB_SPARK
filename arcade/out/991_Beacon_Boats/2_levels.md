NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight pier layout with few boats and slow drift, giving the player simple practice with beacon lighting.
- Level 2 widens the pier and adds one extra beacon, requiring the player to manage lighting across a longer stretch.
- Level 3 increases boat count and adds unlit gaps between beacons, making safe harbour routing harder to plan.
- Level 4 tightens the pier layout with more boats and shorter beacon burn times, forcing faster, more frequent lighting adjustments.
- Level 5 packs the most boats and beacons with the shortest burn times, making accurate continuous lighting management the core challenge.
