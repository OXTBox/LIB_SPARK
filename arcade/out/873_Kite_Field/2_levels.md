NOTES
- Level 1 uses a wide open field with no wind and no birds to learn the basic kite pulling and hoop collection.
- Level 2 adds a gentle wind gust and one slow bird to require steady kite height control while walking.
- Level 3 increases hoop count and adds faster crossing birds to demand quick dodging while balancing the kite line.
- Level 4 tightens the field layout with a short wind gust and more birds to raise the difficulty of timing the kite through hoops.
- Level 5 combines the maximum hoop count, shortest wind gust, and highest bird count for the final challenge of the game.
