NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 add fewer knights and longer crank times, making the timing easier to master.
- Levels 3 and 4 increase the number of knights and narrower moat widths, raising the difficulty of avoiding collisions.
- Level 5 combines the most knights with the shortest crank time and widest moat, requiring precise timing for the hardest challenge.
