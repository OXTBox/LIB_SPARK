NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base speed and three fares on open straight streets, giving the player clear control over each delivery.
- Level 2 raises the speed slightly and adds a single wall block in the middle of the route, forcing the player to steer around obstacles while delivering fares.
- Level 3 increases the fare count to four and adds a second wall cluster, making precise timing harder as the clock shortens faster.
- Level 4 lowers the lives to two and adds tighter wall clusters that require careful acceleration and steering to avoid clock depletion.
- Level 5 raises the speed to the highest value, increases the fare count to five, and adds dense wall clusters to demand fast, precise driving for a high score.
