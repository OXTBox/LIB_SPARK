NOTES
- Level 1 has slow gates and full lives to focus on learning the colour-matching and swap rules.
- Level 2 adds more gates and slightly faster movement to require quicker colour decisions.
- Level 3 reduces lives and adds red gates that block the player unless the current colour matches red.
- Level 4 increases gate speed and reduces lives to demand faster reflexes while managing colour state.
- Level 5 combines the highest gate speed with the fewest lives to require precise colour matching across all gates.
