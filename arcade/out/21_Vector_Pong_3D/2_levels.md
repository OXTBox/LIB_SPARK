NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base ball speed and no obstacles, so the bat can move freely to keep the ball in court.
- Level 2 adds one floating obstacle and a faster computer bat, requiring the player to angle shots off the side wall.
- Level 3 adds a second obstacle and raises the ball speed, forcing the player to use upward shots to stay in court.
- Level 4 adds three obstacles and a faster computer bat, making mid-court bouncing and precise angle shots required.
- Level 5 raises the ball speed and computer bat speed with four obstacles, demanding fast deep-court navigation and precise angle shots.
