NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow vehicles and two cars per lane, giving the player enough time to time each hop without missing traffic.
- Level 2 adds one more car per lane while keeping slow speeds, making lane timing harder as more vehicles must be dodged.
- Level 3 raises vehicle speeds to faster movement, increasing collision risk during each hop.
- Level 4 increases cars per lane to five, creating denser traffic that makes consistent dodging much harder.
- Level 5 uses the fastest vehicle speeds with six cars per lane, creating the most unpredictable traffic and the hardest level to complete.
