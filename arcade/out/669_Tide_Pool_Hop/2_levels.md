LEVELS
level | name | shells (3 to 8) | tide seconds (30 down to 12) | low rocks share percent (20 to 50)
1 | Shore Start | 3 | 30 | 20
2 | Tide Edge | 4 | 26 | 30
3 | Rolling Tide | 5 | 22 | 40
4 | Surging Tide | 6 | 18 | 45
5 | Final Tide | 7 | 12 | 50
NOTES
- Levels 2 to 5 raise the tide speed, reduce the available time, and increase the share of low rocks that get covered early.
- The final level adds more shells to collect while the tide retreats and covers more low rocks, making the hop timing harder.
- Higher low rock shares force faster decisions between safe platforms and covered targets, increasing the challenge each level.
