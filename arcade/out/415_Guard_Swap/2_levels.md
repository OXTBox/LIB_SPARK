NOTES
- Level 1 has one guard that moves slowly and rarely turns, so both heroes can clear the path using knockouts and glaze easily.
- Level 2 adds a second guard that moves through the corridor, requiring the heroes to coordinate knockouts and glaze use to stay safe.
- Level 3 introduces three guards that turn around more often, making it harder to keep both heroes untrapped while reaching the exit.
- Level 4 adds a synchronized gate that requires both heroes to apply glaze to the same guard before the door opens, adding strict coordination.
- Level 5 has five guards that move quickly and turn frequently, demanding precise timing between both tricks to escape the castle.
