NOTES
- Level 1 uses few ledges and slow puff spawning so the up-and-down climb is easy to control.
- Level 2 adds more ledges and slightly faster puff spawning to require steadier upward movement.
- Level 3 increases ledge count and puff speed to demand precise timing while brushing soot-covered steps.
- Level 4 raises puff spawn rate and chimney height to make the climb harder and riskier.
- Level 5 combines the maximum ledge count and highest puff spawn rate for the most challenging climb.
