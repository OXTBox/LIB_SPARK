NOTES
- Level 1 uses two lanes and slow cars so lane changes are simple and easy to control.
- Level 2 adds a third lane and faster cars, requiring more careful lane shifts to avoid braking.
- Level 3 introduces four lanes with faster traffic, making lane coordination harder to manage.
- Level 4 increases traffic density and speed, creating tighter timing challenges for lane changes.
- Level 5 combines the maximum four lanes with the highest traffic rate and speed, making precise lane coordination the hardest task.
