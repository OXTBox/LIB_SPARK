NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow fish and a simple layout to let the diver collect pearls without much pressure.
- Level 2 adds more fish lanes and a faster fish speed to increase the dodging challenge.
- Level 3 extends the layout with more pearl clusters and faster fish to raise the difficulty.
- Level 4 increases fish count and speed while keeping the layout wider to add more dodging pressure.
- Level 5 combines the highest fish count and speed with the longest pearl collection path to make the level hardest.
