NOTES
- Level 1 uses a single cable car and two stations with few hikers to focus on basic lowering timing.
- Level 2 adds a second cable car and three stations, requiring more careful spacing while lowering hikers.
- Level 3 expands to four stations and three cable cars, increasing the risk of cable car collisions on shared spans.
- Level 4 adds a fifth station and keeps three cable cars, demanding precise winch timing across more stations.
- Level 5 keeps five stations and three cable cars with the maximum hiker count, requiring tight coordination of lowering speed and car spacing.
