NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow boats and cars with a fast bridge speed for simple timing practice.
- Level 2 adds more boats and cars while slightly slowing the bridge for tighter timing.
- Level 3 increases both boat and car counts with a slower bridge speed for more complex coordination.
- Level 4 raises the traffic volume further while keeping the bridge speed steady for sustained challenge.
- Level 5 combines the highest traffic volume and slowest bridge speed for the hardest precise timing.
