NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight tunnel with many switches, giving the player plenty of time to learn the light-switching pattern.
- Level 2 adds lateral turns, requiring the player to coordinate light switches with movement direction for longer.
- Level 3 introduces a dual branch layout, forcing the player to choose a path while managing limited light switches.
- Level 4 narrows the corridor and shortens the light glow, making precise timing and quick decisions essential.
- Level 5 packs the most crossings into the shortest light window, demanding rapid, consistent light management throughout.
