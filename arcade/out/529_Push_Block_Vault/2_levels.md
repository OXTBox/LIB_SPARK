NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight open grid with no obstacles, giving players simple practice with block pushing and marked tile filling.
- Level 2 adds a wall column that forces players to route blocks around the obstacle before reaching the marked tiles.
- Level 3 introduces a pit that swallows a pushed block, converting it into floor and reducing available blocks for the puzzle.
- Level 4 tightens the layout by narrowing the walkable space around the pit, forcing careful block placement to avoid losing lives.
- Level 5 combines the tight layout with reduced lives and a higher bonus, requiring precise block routing and efficient puzzle solving for completion.
