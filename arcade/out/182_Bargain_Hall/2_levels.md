NOTES
- Level 1 has all stalls evenly spaced with a long clock for easy trading.
- Level 2 adds a wall blocking part of the market, forcing the player to navigate a tighter path.
- Level 3 increases stall density and adds a second wall cluster to slow trading movement.
- Level 4 raises stall density further with more wall clusters, making stall navigation harder.
- Level 5 packs stalls tightly together with the shortest clock, creating the highest difficulty.
