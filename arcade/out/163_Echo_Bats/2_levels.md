NOTES
- Level 1 has few bats at slow speed and a wide open cave, so the player can practice standing still without much risk.
- Level 2 adds one more bat and slightly faster movement, requiring the player to adjust movement timing more carefully.
- Level 3 introduces more complex wall layouts that force bats to change direction more often, increasing swoop risk.
- Level 4 packs five bats at faster speed into a tighter cave, making dodging swoops much harder.
- Level 5 combines six fast bats in a compact cave layout, presenting the highest difficulty for the player to complete the level safely.
