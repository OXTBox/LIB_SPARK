NOTES
- Level 1 uses the base ball speed and three brick rows, giving easy control for the bat to guide the ball safely while clearing the wall.
- Level 2 increases the ball speed and adds one more brick row, making the ball harder to keep in play without losing a life.
- Level 3 adds strong bricks that require two hits to destroy, increasing the difficulty of clearing the wall efficiently.
- Level 4 raises the ball speed further and adds two more brick rows, requiring faster bat coordination to prevent the ball from falling past the bat.
- Level 5 combines the highest ball speed, the most brick rows, and the most complex layout, making precise bat and ball coordination the hardest challenge.
