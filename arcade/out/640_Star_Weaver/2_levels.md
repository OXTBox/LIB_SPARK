- 2 to 4 lines: each level adds more required connections, tighter star spacing, and a shorter time limit to increase difficulty.

- Level 2 increases the shape to 5 stars with more extra stars, keeping the time limit stable for moderate challenge.
- Level 3 expands the shape to 6 stars with more extra stars and reduces the time limit slightly to raise difficulty.
- Level 4 adds 7 required shape stars with more extra stars and further reduces the time limit for harder alignment.
- Level 5 requires 8 shape stars, the most extra stars, and the shortest time limit for the hardest level.
