LEVELS
level | name | marbles (2 to 8) | playground size cells (8 to 14) | bell seconds (60 down to 30)
1 | First Trail | 2 | 8 | 60
2 | Curved Loop | 4 | 10 | 60
3 | Tight Corner | 6 | 12 | 55
4 | Wide Spiral | 8 | 14 | 50
5 | Final Enclosure | 8 | 14 | 30
NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight playground with two marbles close together, giving easy timing for the first chalk loop.
- Level 2 adds a gentle curved layout that requires the chalk line to follow a slight arc, increasing spatial control needed.
- Level 3 tightens the playground space and adds more marbles close together, forcing quick, precise loop adjustments.
- Level 4 expands the playground with a wide spiral layout and eight marbles spread across the area, demanding steady chalk line tracking.
- Level 5 keeps the largest playground and most marbles while reducing the bell time to 30 seconds, making the final enclosure the hardest challenge.
