NOTES
- Level 1 uses slow log speed and few hazards to let the player learn basic jumps and vine swings.
- Level 2 adds more pits and a vine to require careful water navigation while logs move faster.
- Level 3 increases log count and speed to demand precise timing during the crossing.
- Level 4 raises hazard density and log speed to push the player toward the final vine swing.
- Level 5 combines the highest hazard density and fastest log speed to test full survival and collection.
