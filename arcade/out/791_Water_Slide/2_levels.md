NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight, gentle path with few rings to let players learn the left and right controls.
- Level 2 adds a moderate winding curve and more rings to build steady control without extreme speed changes.
- Level 3 increases the bend density and ring count to require tighter balance while the slide speeds up.
- Level 4 raises the bend complexity and speed to demand precise steering to avoid crossing the slide edge.
- Level 5 combines the highest bend density and fastest slide speed to make safe navigation the hardest challenge.
