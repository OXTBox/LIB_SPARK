NOTES
- Level 1 uses a small 8-cell maze with two mirrors and one button pair to teach the basic swap mechanic.
- Level 2 expands the maze to 10 cells and adds a third mirror to require more careful path planning before swapping.
- Level 3 doubles the maze size to 12 cells and adds two button pairs, demanding precise mirror coordination for both targets.
- Level 4 raises the maze to 14 cells with six mirrors to create complex reflection paths that require sustained planning.
- Level 5 keeps the largest 14-cell maze but adds eight mirrors and three button pairs, making the spatial mirror puzzle the hardest to solve.
