NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use short corridors with sparse bumpers to let the player focus on basic star collection.
- Levels 3 and 4 add closer bumpers and more drains to increase wall navigation difficulty.
- Level 5 combines the longest corridor with the most bumpers and drains for the hardest arcade challenge.
