NOTES
- Levels 2 to 5 increase the wall length required, reduce the time before the snowball volley starts, and raise the number of incoming snowballs for harder defense.
- Later levels place the fort closer to the snowy yard edge, forcing the player to build the wall faster and with tighter positioning to block every incoming snowball.
