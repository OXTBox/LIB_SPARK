LEVELS
level | name | dominoes (8 to 30) | gaps (1 to 6) | par moves (6 to 20)

5 | Full Chain | 26 | 5 | 20
NOTES
- Level 1 uses short straight paths and few standing dominoes to teach the basic tip-and-chain movement.
- Level 2 adds longer gaps between dominoes to require steady forward movement while keeping the chain simple.
- Level 3 introduces side-straight dominoes that must be walked into to create safe paths through the chain.
- Level 4 increases domino density and chain length to demand precise timing while approaching the bell.
- Level 5 combines dense domino layouts, multiple safe gaps, and a long chain to test full chain transfer accuracy.
