NOTES
- Level 1 uses fewer holes and slower moles so players can learn the timing mechanic without pressure.
- Level 2 adds more holes and slightly faster moles to increase the speed of the timing challenge.
- Level 3 narrows the lawn space and raises mole speed to force quicker, more precise positioning.
- Level 4 packs the most holes and fastest moles, making safe positioning much harder to maintain.
- Level 5 keeps the maximum hole and mole density while the shortest mole up time creates the highest difficulty.
