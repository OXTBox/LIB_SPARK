NOTES
- Level 1 is the easiest: the puck moves slowly and the rival goal is wide, so the player only needs basic wall bounces to score.
- Level 2 raises the puck speed and the rival craft moves faster, so the player must time bounces more precisely to keep the puck in play.
- Level 3 shrinks the rival goal slightly and increases the rival craft speed, making scoring harder as the puck must be pushed into a smaller target.
- Level 4 tightens the field walls and raises the puck speed further, so the puck bounces away more often and the player must time every move carefully.
- Level 5 raises the final speed and makes the rival goal the smallest, requiring exact timing of every bounce to score before the rival closes its goal.
