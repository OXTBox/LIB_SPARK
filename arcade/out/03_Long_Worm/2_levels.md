NOTES
- Level 1 uses a small open grid with few walls and few foods, letting the worm grow slowly without early danger.
- Level 2 adds more food cells while keeping the open layout, so the worm grows faster but still has a clear path to follow.
- Level 3 introduces corner walls that block direct straight paths, forcing the worm to turn and risk hitting its own trail.
- Level 4 adds a food cell inside the lower area, creating a tight space where the worm must navigate carefully to avoid its own body.
- Level 5 adds more walls and food across the full grid, making the maze complex and requiring precise turns to stay alive while growing.
