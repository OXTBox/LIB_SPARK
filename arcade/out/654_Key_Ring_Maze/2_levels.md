- Level 1 uses a single key and one lock, so the player learns the basic swap and unlock mechanics with a simple path.
- Level 2 adds a second key and two locks, requiring the player to carry a key across a corridor to unlock the next section.
- Level 3 adds a third key and three locks, with locks placed on opposite sides of the maze forcing the player to collect keys in a specific order.
- Level 4 increases wall density and narrower corridors, making movement slower and the key collection sequence harder to plan.
- Level 5 adds the exit door and the most complex colour chain, requiring the player to unlock all three locks in sequence before reaching the exit.
