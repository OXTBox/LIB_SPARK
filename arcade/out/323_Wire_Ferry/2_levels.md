NOTES
- Level 1 has only one crate and a slow current, giving the player enough time to learn the balance controls.
- Level 2 adds a second crate and a stronger current push, requiring more precise weight management while crossing.
- Level 3 increases the crate count to three and raises the current speed, making the clock pressure harder to manage.
- Level 4 adds a narrower right-side path that restricts safe movement space, increasing the risk of unbalanced tips.
- Level 5 combines five crates, a strong current, and a shorter clock duration, demanding maximum speed and perfect balance control.
