NOTES
- Level 1 uses a short bridge and no wind to let the player learn the planer and carrier swap.
- Level 2 widens the ravine and adds light wind to require more careful plank placement.
- Level 3 adds more planks and stronger wind to raise the coordination difficulty.
- Level 4 increases the bridge length and wind to demand precise timing between planer and carrier.
- Level 5 uses the maximum planks and highest wind to test full bridge support and stable movement.
