NOTES
- Level 1 has a small maze with few mushrooms and a wide light radius, giving the player easy control over wall breaks.
- Level 2 widens the maze and adds one mushroom, requiring the player to time their wall breaks more carefully.
- Level 3 increases mushroom count and maze size, making bigger mushrooms and double wall breaks harder to time.
- Level 4 shrinks the light radius and adds more mushrooms, increasing the risk of bumping the stone without reaching it.
- Level 5 uses the largest maze with the fewest light radius visibility, making wall timing and avoiding the stone the hardest challenge.
