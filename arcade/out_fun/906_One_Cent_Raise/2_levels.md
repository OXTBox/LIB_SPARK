NOTES
- Level 1 has a simple open path with one slow box and no truck bays, giving the worker easy control over box pushes.
- Level 2 adds a first delivery tower and two stacked slow boxes, requiring the worker to push boxes aside before clearing storage rooms.
- Level 3 introduces more delivery towers and three stacked slow boxes, with two truck bays to manage box returns.
- Level 4 adds five delivery colours and hidden letter boxes, increasing the difficulty of finding rare letters while dodging more towers.
- Level 5 has the highest delivery rate and the most complex layered box stacking, demanding precise timing to clear all boxes and match letters.
