NOTES
- Level 1 uses a straight hallway with one sentry and few doors, letting the survivor learn the door mechanics.
- Level 2 widens the corridor and adds a second sentry, increasing the risk of touching guards while moving.
- Level 3 introduces a winding layout with more doors and three sentries, forcing careful navigation through unexpected rooms.
- Level 4 adds more survivors wandering freely and faster sentries, making the hallway harder to navigate without losing lives.
- Level 5 packs the maximum survivors, sentries, and doors into a tight layout, making survival the hardest challenge.
