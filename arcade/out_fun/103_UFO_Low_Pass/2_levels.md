NOTES
- Level 1 has no power lines or water towers, so the UFO glides freely and the beam reaches easily.
- Level 2 adds one power line and more guns, making the beam path narrower and the UFO more dangerous.
- Level 3 introduces a water tower that blocks the beam path, forcing the player to climb higher to find a gap.
- Level 4 raises the number of obstacles and guns, requiring precise steering to keep the beam reachable.
- Level 5 combines all obstacles, the highest gun count, and the most people to collect, making the path the most difficult.
