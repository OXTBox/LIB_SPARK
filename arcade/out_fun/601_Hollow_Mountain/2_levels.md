NOTES
- Level 1 has a single floor and one spider, giving the miner a wide, slow path to practice moving and dropping.
- Level 2 adds a second floor and a faster ghost, forcing the miner to manage the flashlight battery while clearing the path.
- Level 3 introduces hanging threads with three spiders, increasing the risk of catching the miner while climbing.
- Level 4 raises the ghost speed and adds more threads, making the ghost harder to keep away from the walls and floors.
- Level 5 adds the most floors and the fastest ghost with five spiders, pushing the miner to balance battery use, ghost avoidance, and spider evasion at once.
