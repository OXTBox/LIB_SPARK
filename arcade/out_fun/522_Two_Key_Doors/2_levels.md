NOTES
- Level 1 uses a simple straight maze with one plate to teach the swap and door-opening rules without pressure.
- Level 2 adds a second plate and a wider maze, requiring the player to manage both heroes' paths without timed doors.
- Level 3 introduces the first timed door, adding a pause mechanic while the maze grows wider and plates multiply.
- Level 4 adds more timed doors and the largest maze, forcing careful timing of hero swaps and plate usage.
- Level 5 combines all mechanics with the most plates and timed doors, making the level the hardest in the set.
