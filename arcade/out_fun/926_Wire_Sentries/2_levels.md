NOTES
- Level 1 has one slow sentry and a wide open maze, so the player can collect all cores without worrying about sight lines.
- Level 2 adds a second sentry that patrols a parallel corridor, forcing the player to plan routes between cores carefully.
- Level 3 introduces mid-loop sentry direction changes, so planned safe routes suddenly become direct sight lines.
- Level 4 packs four sentries into the maze, making sight-line detection harder and increasing the risk of being caught.
- Level 5 uses the fastest sentry speed with all six sentries, making the maze tight and sight-line evasion the hardest challenge.
