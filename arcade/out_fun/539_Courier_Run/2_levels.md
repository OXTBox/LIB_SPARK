NOTES
- Level 1 uses simple straight street paths with one slow dog to teach basic parcel pickup and movement.
- Level 2 adds a looping street layout and a second dog to require more careful path planning.
- Level 3 introduces a crossing layout that forces the player to dodge multiple dogs at once.
- Level 4 increases the number of parcels and dogs while raising the dog speed to create tighter dodging challenges.
- Level 5 packs all 8 parcels into a complex maze with the fastest dog and the shortest clock for the hardest challenge.
