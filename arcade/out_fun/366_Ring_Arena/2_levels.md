NOTES
- Levels 1 and 2 use fewer drones and slower drift to let the player charge the shockwave without rushing.
- Level 3 adds more drones and slightly faster drift to require careful positioning while charging.
- Levels 4 and 5 push drone speed to the maximum and keep the highest drone count to make clearing the final waves harder.
- Every level keeps the same circular arena layout and shockwave rules, only changing drone count, speed, and lives.
